# Tetris Supervisor Manager PRD ## Overview Tetris Supervisor Manager turns the user's request into a directly usable application. The first screen is not a landing page; it is the real workflow surface where the user can inspect data, take actions, and recover from empty or error states. ## Screens to Generate 1. **Game Board**: Main gameplay area with 10x20 grid, HUD (Score, Level, Lines), Next piece preview, and Hold slot. 2. **Main Menu**: Entry point with "New Game", "Options", and "How to Play". 3. **Pause Overlay**: Modal-style overlay with "Resume", "Restart", and "Quit to Menu". 4. **Game Over**: Result screen with final score, high score, and "Play Again" button. 5. **Controls Help**: Informational screen showing keyboard mappings (Arrows, Space, Shift, P). 6. **Game Options**: Settings for Sound, Ghost Piece toggle, and Difficulty/Starting Level.
